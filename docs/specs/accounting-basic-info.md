# Accounting Basic Info (FF-094)

Source: Francis Santos, email Nov 17 2026, 1:37 PM ET (Gmail message 1a1eacd7648484d9), item 3. Tracker FF-094. Medium.

## What was asked

On Accounting, rename the Contract Figures section to Basic Info. Keep Sales Price, Loan Amount, Closing Date, Disbursement Date, and Source of Business. Take these off that section: How It Was Obtained, Source for This Figure, Seller Credit, the duplicate Earnest Money block at the bottom, Warranty and HOA / Condo / Deferred, and the History tax section. Do not drop the stored columns. Other screens that already read those values keep working.

## What it does

The Accounting menu and the page heading say Basic Info. The form shows the five figures from the order, the same ones Place Order and Edit order details already save. Saving writes those order fields. It does not open the old contract-term sheet, so a blank box cannot wipe a warranty, an HOA name, a tax levy, or a seller credit that was saved before.

Earnest money stays as its own card above the form. That is the copy shared with Add New Order. The second earnest-money block that used to sit at the bottom of the contract sheet is gone. Commissions stay as the read-only Agent fees summary.

A refinance has no sales price on this form, matching Edit order details. Sales, the sales team, and a transaction coordinator can read the form and cannot change it.

The old menu link that also said Basic Info and opened the file overview is now labeled Order overview, so the menu does not list Basic Info twice.

## What is not in this change

Other Charges (section H) can take a home-warranty charge and the HOA, condo, and deferred-water lines that are paid at closing. It does not hold the old contract-sheet fields: warranty cap and payer, HOA or condo name, monthly dues, certificate balance and date, or a general deferred amount, schedule, and payee. Those values stay in the database and are no longer on this screen.

Seller credit stays under Charges, L/N. Taxes stay under Taxes & Prorations. No database file. Not Live until someone checks it on TEST.

TC Contract info is a different screen. It still says Contract info and still has sale price, contract date, seller concessions, settlement date, and earnest money. The role walkthrough does not look for the words "Contract figures".
