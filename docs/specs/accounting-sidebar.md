# Accounting sidebar (FF-061)

Source: Francis Santos, Nov 12 2026, 1:53 PM ET (Gmail 1a1d11bd4bd135f8): make Closing and Accounting two independent sections in the left sidebar. She confirmed yes on Nov 17 2026, 1:55 PM ET (Gmail 1a1eaddb3dcfc5d6) and named the Accounting pages. Tracker row FF-061.

## What the user sees

On a file, the Closing menu is unchanged. That is Scheduling Details on the file sidebar, Closing appointment for a buyer or seller, and the Closing charges list (A through N) on the accounting page.

Accounting is its own section below that menu. The links, in order, are:

1. Ledger
2. Receipts
3. Disbursements
4. Account Balances
5. Balancing
6. Loan Funding

The same six links are on the accounting page, directly under the Closing charges and above Preview.

Ledger is the fee ledger that used to sit under CLA tools. Receipts, Disbursements, Account Balances, and Balancing read that same test ledger. Loan Funding opens the Funding page that already exists.

## Who can see it

The section uses the same rule as the old Accounting link: the Accounting module is on, and the person's role can read Accounting. A buyer, seller, or agent does not see it. A Funding Team login that cannot read Accounting still sees Funding where it was, labeled Funding, and does not see the Accounting section.

Loan Funding is shown inside Accounting only when that person could already open Funding. The Funding page still refuses anyone it refused before.

## What it does not do

These pages do not show a bank balance, take a deposit, print a check, or send a wire. They do not change the address of Accounting or Funding. They do not add a database change.
