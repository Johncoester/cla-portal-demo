# Mortgage brokerage contact (FF-090)

Source: Francis Santos, email Nov 16 2026, 3:10 PM ET (Gmail msg 1a1e5fbfe4183a9f), tracker row 326 (FF-090). Medium priority. The same email also asks for the Transaction Coordinator error (FF-088), Commission Paid By (FF-089), and the Taxes and Fees document list (FF-091). Those are separate rows.

## What was asked

On the file's Mortgage Brokerage section, add the brokerage company name and its contact information: contact name, phone, email, and address.

If the portal already has a contacts lookup, use it the way the lender fields do, so a broker can be picked or added. Otherwise use plain fields.

The values save on the file, come back when the file is opened again, and only staff who can edit the file can change them.

Show the broker on the Settlement Statement and on Closing Disclosure page 5, in the Mortgage Broker column of Contact Information.

## What it does

On a file's Contacts tab, under Loan, Mortgage Brokerage shows the company and, when they have been entered, the contact name, phone, email, and address.

Edit Mortgage Brokerage opens those fields. A list offers mortgage brokers and brokerage companies already saved, the same kind of list the lender block uses. Choosing a person fills the company, contact name, phone, and email. The address is typed on this file, because a saved contact does not keep an address. Choosing a company fills the company name. Not listed means type the fields.

Save writes the five values on the file. Picking a saved broker, or typing a new contact name, also puts that person on the file as a mortgage broker. A second broker can still be added under Mortgage Broker. Switching the brokerage starts from blank fields. Brokers already on the file stay until someone removes them.

Place Order asks for the same company and contact fields. They are optional. A phone that is not 10 digits, an email that is not valid, or contact information with no company name is pointed out in plain words, and what was typed stays.

Someone who cannot edit the file sees the company and the contact, with no buttons. A buyer and a salesperson cannot save a change.

The Settlement Statement header names the mortgage broker when any of those values are on the file. Closing Disclosure page 5, Contact Information, Mortgage Broker column, shows the company as the name, the address, the contact, the email, and the phone. If the file has no company, a name typed only in CD details still shows.

## What is not in this change

The database file is not applied on TEST. Until it is, saving a brokerage contact on TEST says the file setup is not finished and keeps what was typed. Saving only the company name, with the contact fields left blank, does not need the new columns.

NMLS and license numbers for the mortgage broker are not collected. The CD column leaves those as a dash.
