## What the user sees

On CRM, then Companies, the All Companies list sorts from the column headers, the same way All Leads, All Clients, and All Deals already do.

Click a header once to sort A to Z, or smallest to largest. Click the same header again to reverse it. The lit arrow shows which column is sorted and which way. The other arrows stay dim.

The list still opens on Company Name, A to Z.

The Sort menu under the filters is gone. Search, Company Owner, Type, Archived, Created Time, Modified Time, and Last Activity Time still narrow the list. The sort applies to the companies that match, and the next page continues that same order.

A sort already saved for this login is used when the list opens. Reset clears the search and the filters. It keeps the column you sorted.

## Columns

Company Name, Type, City, Company Owner, Clients, Orders, Volume, Created Time, Modified Time, and Last Activity Time. A column added from Manage columns sorts the same way.

City sorts the city and state shown in that column. Blank city, blank owner, and blank times stay at the bottom either way. Clients, Orders, and Volume sort as amounts. Type sorts the stored type.

Archived companies stay hidden until Archived is set to show them.

## What this does not change

Who can see a company. The company form. Leads, Clients, and Deals. Reports and custom views. No new database file.
