# Aggregate adjustment (FF-096)

Source: Francis Santos, email "Escrow Section", Nov 17 2026, 2:20 PM ET (Gmail message 1a1eaf4ac2ad6496). Tracker FF-096. She was Processor 2 on TEST.

## What was asked

On Escrow (initial escrow payment at closing, CD section G), an aggregate adjustment of -$1,200 must come off the total. It must also come off the borrower's totals and every Closing Disclosure, settlement statement, and ledger figure that uses that line.

Staff type it with a minus sign, with parentheses, or with a dollar sign and commas.

## What it does

Escrow line 08, Aggregate adjustment, is a borrower charge at closing. The amount is kept as a positive number. The line is marked so it lowers the total.

These all lower the total by $1,200.00:

- -$1,200
- -$1,200.00
- $-1,200.00
- ($1,200.00)
- (1,200.00)
- $1,200.00
- 1,200.00

A blank box or $0.00 clears the adjustment and does not lower the total. A value that is not an amount stays in the box and says to enter it as -$1,200.00, ($1,200.00), or $1,200.00.

On Francis's example, homeowner's insurance $1,368.00 plus property taxes $3,568.00 minus the aggregate adjustment $1,200.00 is an Escrow total of $3,736.00. Before this change the total was $6,136.00.

The same $1,200.00 comes off the borrower's debits, the amount due from the borrower, CD section G, CD closing costs, cash to close, and the settlement statement.
