## What was asked

Under Earnest and Commission, show Listing Agent Commission and Selling Agent Commission as two panels, side by side, instead of one combined commission.

Each panel has Commission base × Commission %, plus Additional amount, equaling Commission amount, and Commission paid by Buyer, Seller, or Split. Split means a dollar amount from the buyer and a dollar amount from the seller.

Option 1: use that layout as the design of the Agent fees box, one panel per side. Commission base defaults to the sales price. The percent and the dollar amount stay in step. The additional amount is added on.

## What it does

On a file, the Agent fees box shows the two panels next to each other on a computer and one under the other on a phone. The selling agent panel is the buyer's agent side.

A blank commission base uses the sales price. Typing the percent fills in the commission amount. Typing the commission amount fills in the percent. The additional amount is added on top. Paid by can be Buyer, Seller, or Split. The two split amounts must add up to the commission amount.

The admin fee and the other fees stay on each panel. Buyers and sellers still do not see the box.

## What is not in this change

A split commission, or a commission with its own base, is not rewritten when the sales price changes. Open the panel and save it again.

The old Commission information task still does not take a commission amount. License ID and email recipients are not part of this change.

The database file must be applied on TEST before a custom base, an additional amount, or a split can be saved there.
