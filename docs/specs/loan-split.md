# Loan split (FF-053)

Source: Francis, tracker row 184 (Requests). Francis confirmed the numbered list by email on Nov 11 2026, 2:37 PM ET ("That's correct"). John Coester asked for it as the next weekend item on Nov 15 2026.

## What was asked

Under Loan, split the one Mortgage Broker / Brokerage box into two sections.

1. Mortgage Brokerage shows the brokerage company, with Edit Mortgage Brokerage and Switch Mortgage Brokerage.
2. Mortgage Broker shows the individual broker's contact information, lets staff search and select existing brokers at that brokerage, and has Add Mortgage Broker to create a new contact. One brokerage can have more than one broker.

## What it does

On a file's Contacts tab, staff who can edit the file see Mortgage Brokerage and Mortgage Broker under Loan. The brokerage is the company name. Edit opens that name. Switch starts from a blank name. Brokers already on the file stay when the company name changes.

Mortgage Broker lists each broker's name, phone, and email. Search only offers brokers already saved at that brokerage. Add Mortgage Broker creates a contact at that company and puts the person on the file. A second broker can be added the same way.

Place Order still asks for the brokerage company name only. The loan officer and lender processor stay in Lender contacts.

A blank brokerage, a blank broker name, or an email that is not valid is pointed out in plain words, and what was typed stays on the form.

Someone who cannot edit the file sees the brokerage name and the brokers, with no buttons.

## What is not in this change

The brokerage does not have its own phone, email, or address. Those were not part of the confirmed list. The company information stored today is the name.

Switching the brokerage does not remove brokers already on the file.

The database file must be applied on TEST before a mortgage broker contact can be saved there. Until then, TEST still only allows Loan Officer and Lender TC.
